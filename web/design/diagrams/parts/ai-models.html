Models

Must get across: a model is metadata and a provider holds the credentials. The binding pairs them and gets one function for each interface that the provider implements.

Today the first flowchart draws the three functions as equal boxes under the binding, so "always" and "only if" look the same. The second draws the wrapper as nested groups, with no call that goes through them.
